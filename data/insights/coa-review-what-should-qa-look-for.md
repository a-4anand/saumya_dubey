Certificate of Analysis review is often treated as a routine task, but routine does not mean automatic. A careful review helps confirm whether the document is complete, consistent and appropriate within the agreed documentation framework.

## Start with completeness

Check whether the expected identifiers, results, references and approvals are present. A missing field can create disproportionate follow-up later.

## Look for internal consistency

The COA should make sense within itself. Values, units, product identifiers and approval details should be clear and consistent.

## Check alignment with related documentation

A COA does not exist in isolation. It may need to align with specifications, batch records or the relevant review context.

## Review readability

Even a technically complete document can be hard to interpret. Clear formatting and consistent structure matter because they reduce ambiguity during later review.

## Escalate what cannot be comfortably explained

If a result, format issue or discrepancy needs repeated interpretation, it likely needs clarification before final acceptance.

Good COA review helps maintain a cleaner documentation trail and supports more confident downstream QA decisions.
